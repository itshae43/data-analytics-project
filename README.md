# Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Sample Superstore dataset to identify
sales trends, profitability patterns, regional performance, and customer
segment performance.

## Dataset

- Dataset: Sample Superstore
- Domain: Sales / E-commerce
- Records after cleaning: 9,983
- Columns: 21

## Data Preparation

The dataset was inspected and cleaned using Python and Pandas.

Cleaning activities included:

- Data inspection
- Missing-value identification
- Missing-value handling
- Duplicate checking
- Data validation
- Date validation

## Exploratory Data Analysis

EDA was performed to analyze:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Discount
- Category
- Region
- Customer Segment
- Sales trends

## Data Visualization

The project includes visualizations for:

- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales vs Profit
- Profit by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment

## Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created containing:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Category filter
- Region filter
- Segment filter

## Business Insights

- Technology has the highest sales and profit.
- Furniture has the lowest profit among the categories.
- West has the highest regional sales.
- Consumer is the largest customer segment by sales.
- Sales and profit have a moderate positive relationship.
- Discount has a negative relationship with profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Continue focusing on the Technology category.
- Improve Furniture profitability.
- Review and control discount levels.
- Investigate opportunities to improve South region performance.
